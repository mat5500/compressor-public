Privacy Policy

Compressor: Photo & Video · Last updated 24 October 2026
Short version: your photos and videos never leave your device. Compressor has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Your photos and videos

All compression runs locally on your iPhone, iPad or Mac using Apple's built-in frameworks. Files you pick, their names, contents and metadata (including location) are never uploaded, shared or seen by us. Compressed copies are stored only in the app's own folder on your device (also visible in the Files app) until you delete them. Your originals are never changed.

Photo library

The app reads only the photos and videos you choose in the system photo picker. When you tap Save to Photos, it asks for permission to add the compressed copies to your library. It never reads, scans or uploads your library.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. To record and restore purchases we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user identifier and the App Store transaction details (product, price, date, country). This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

No contact details, no location, no contacts, no browsing history, no usage analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reports sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete compressed files in the app (Settings › Delete All Compressed Files) or delete the app. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat contain no personal content; contact us to ask about them.
