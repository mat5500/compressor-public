Common questions

Where are my compressed files?
In the app's Library tab, and in the Files app under On My iPhone (or iPad) › Compressor. Tap Save to Photos to add them to your library.
Does it free up space on my iPhone?
Compressor makes smaller copies and never deletes your originals. After you save the copies to Photos and check them, delete the originals in Photos (and empty Recently Deleted) to get the space back.
What does Free include?
Five files a day, one at a time, with videos up to one minute long. All quality levels and formats are included.
What does Pro add?
Batch compression of many photos and videos at once, no daily limit, videos of any length and removing location and camera data. It's a one-time purchase, with no subscription.
I bought Pro on another device.
Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
Which video format should I pick?
HEVC gives the smallest files and plays on Apple devices and most modern phones. Pick H.264 if the video must play on older Windows PCs or websites.
Is anything uploaded?
No. Every compression runs on your device. See the privacy policy.
